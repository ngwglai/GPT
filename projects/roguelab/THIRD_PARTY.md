## Chinese font (v1.1.0)

The bundled `assets/fonts/NotoSansSC.woff2` is a subset of Noto Sans CJK SC Regular (Noto Sans CJK Regular collection, SC face), renamed **RogueLab CJK**. Upstream: <https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk>. It retains GB2312 characters and every localized UI glyph. It is redistributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; the complete copyright and license notice is in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt` and the Windows package's `FONT-LICENSE.txt`. This permits bundling and redistribution with open-source games.
